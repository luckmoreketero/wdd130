Why Paddle With Rapid Rush

Our Purpose

Rapid Rush Rafting was started to share the power and beauty of the Zambezi River with the world. Whether it is your first paddle stroke or your fiftieth rapid, our local guides turn a day on the water into a story you will keep telling.

What We Offer

  • Scenic floats and heart-pounding Class V rapids
  • All gear provided: helmet, life jacket and paddle
  • Small groups and personal attention
  • Half-day and full-day trips

Safety Is Our Priority

Every trip begins with a full safety talk and paddle practice. Our guides are trained in swift-water rescue and first aid, and we check river conditions before every launch.

From gentle floats to the wildest rapids on earth, there is a trip waiting for you. Compare our trips, pick your thrill level and secure your spot before the season fills up.
