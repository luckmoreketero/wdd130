Rapid Rush Rafting

Our purpose is to bring adventure, joy, and unforgettable memories to everyone who loves the river. Our mission is to provide safe, thrilling, and affordable white water rafting experiences. Our motto: Paddle Hard, Smile Harder!

History

Founded in 1998 by a group of river enthusiasts, Rapid Rush started with one raft and a big dream. Today we have grown to be one of the most trusted rafting companies.

We have guided thousands of happy adventurers every year. We believe in safety, fun, and respect for the river and nature.
