Featured Trips

Zambezi Scenic Float

Class I-II • Beginner

A relaxed paddle above the falls with time to spot hippos, birds and the spray cloud on the horizon. Ideal for families and first-timers.

  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Minimum age: 8
  • Price: $65 per person

Gorge Half-Day Run

Class III-IV • Intermediate

Descend into the Batoka Gorge for a morning of steady rapids, big waves and plenty of teamwork, finishing with a hearty lunch on the riverbank.

  • Duration: 5 hours
  • Minimum age: 12
  • Price: $110 per person

Rapid Rush Extreme

Class V • Advanced

Our signature full-day run through the biggest commercially rafted white water in the world. For confident swimmers who want the full adrenaline experience.

  • Duration: 8 hours (lunch included)
  • Minimum age: 15
  • Price: $165 per person
